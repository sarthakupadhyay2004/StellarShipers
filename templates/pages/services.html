Sourcing Services

From requirement to reliable supply.

STELLAR SHIPERS helps international B2B buyers source selected natural fibers and sustainable materials from India according to their product requirements. We coordinate with suitable Indian producers, clarify specifications, oversee sourcing and prepare material for international supply.

Our Sourcing Process

Structured 5-Stage Sourcing Coordination

STAGE 01 Requirement

Requirement-Based Sourcing

Share your product requirement, approximate quantity, specifications and intended application. We evaluate the requirement and identify a suitable sourcing route in India.

STAGE 02 Coordination

Supplier Coordination

We coordinate with selected Indian producers regarding product availability, agreed specifications, quantity, production schedule and packaging requirements.

STAGE 03 Verification

Quality & Specification Control

We work according to the specifications agreed with the buyer. Where practical, material is reviewed before dispatch. If additional testing or verification is required, it can be arranged according to the specific order requirement.

STAGE 04 Preparation

Product Preparation

We coordinate preparation according to the agreed order requirements, including required form or dimensions, appropriate packaging and buyer-specific preparation where technically feasible.

STAGE 05 Export

Export Coordination

Once the material is approved for shipment, we coordinate the necessary export arrangements and documentation according to the agreed order and destination requirements.

Our Approach

Straightforward, Accountability-Focused Procurement

We keep sourcing straightforward: understand the requirement, identify the right supply, coordinate the agreed specifications, and support the order through export.
