B2B Commercial Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. This policy outlines how STELLAR SHIPERS collects, processes, and protects corporate and commercial sourcing data submitted across our wholesale gateway.

1. Scope of B2B Commercial Data Processing

STELLAR SHIPERS operates strictly as a business-to-business (B2B) international sourcing and export entity. We do not provide consumer e-commerce services, nor do we solicit personal consumer transactions. Information submitted through our portal (such as corporate representative names, corporate email addresses, phone numbers, technical specifications, and enterprise details) is gathered solely for commercial inquiry evaluation, Request for Quotation (RFQ) processing, and international export logistics execution.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information submitted directly by authorized procurement agents and representatives:

  • Enterprise Identifiers: Corporate entity name, registered country, operational headquarters.
  • Commercial Contact Data: Representative name, corporate email address, direct telephone / WhatsApp.
  • Sourcing Specifications: Target raw material, required volume, intended application, technical drawing attachments, packaging requirements, destination port.
  • Technical Log Data: IP address, browser type, and submission timestamps recorded for cybersecurity and anti-spam verification.

3. Commercial Purpose of Use

We process collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Evaluating raw material availability, mill capacity, and laboratory testing requirements.
  • Formulating formal Requests for Quotations (RFQs) and proforma commercial invoices.
  • Dispatching laboratory sample evaluation kits and technical data sheets (TDS).
  • Fulfilling mandatory statutory export and customs regulations (such as Bill of Lading, Phytosanitary certification, and Certificate of Origin declarations).

4. Technical Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We treat all proprietary compounding requirements, engineering specifications, and CAD drawings uploaded by buyers as strictly confidential trade secrets. We never sell, monetize, or disclose client technical requirements to unauthorized third parties. Disclosure is limited strictly to accredited testing laboratories, ocean freight carriers, and statutory customs authorities strictly necessary to fulfill trade obligations.
