Operational Sourcing & Quality Control Model

The 6-Stage Quality & Export Model

Supplier produces → STELLAR SHIPERS verifies → approved material is exported → buyer receives professional communication.

Specification Integrity Standard

“Do not describe supplier statements as independently verified facts.”

Current business model is primarily sourcing + quality control + export coordination. Supplier production is external. We do not invent or imply owned factories or laboratories. We personally review supplier output, conduct quality verification prior to dispatch, and coordinate independent testing according to buyer-specified tolerances.

Detailed Stage Breakdown

STAGE 01 Origin

Carefully selected sourcing network

Role: Identify suitable Indian producers and production capability with verified regional feedstock (such as Jalgaon, Maharashtra for banana fiber).

STAGE 02 Alignment

Requirement-led sourcing

Role: Deeply understand the buyer's application (textile, spinning, non-woven), target technical specifications, volume, and commercial requirements.

STAGE 03 Inspection

Quality verification before export

Role: Personally review supplier output before approval where practical, confirming cleanliness, fiber length consistency, and absence of chemical treatment.

STAGE 04 QC Gate

Non-conforming material is not approved

Role: If material fails to meet required parameters, request correction/reproduction from the producer or allocate another qualified producer in our network.

STAGE 05 Interface

One professional point of contact

Role: Manage all buyer commercial communication, reasonable customization, moisture barrier packaging, and sample logistics through a single desk.

STAGE 06 Export

Responsible export coordination

Role: Coordinate approved orders, phytosanitary inspection, customs export documentation, Bill of Lading, and ocean freight logistics to European ports.
