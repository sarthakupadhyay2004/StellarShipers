Company Story

Bridging Indian Production with Global B2B Markets

STELLAR SHIPERS was built around a simple belief: capable Indian producers should have better access to international markets, and global buyers should have dependable partners who can bridge sourcing and execution.

We work to connect carefully selected Indian production capabilities with international B2B buyers. Our role goes beyond arranging a product. We coordinate sourcing, review product quality, communicate requirements, support reasonable customization and coordinate the export process.

Our long-term objective is to build a reliable Indian export house connecting regional producers with international industrial converters.

Operating Model

How STELLAR SHIPERS Operates

Supplier produces → STELLAR SHIPERS verifies → approved material is exported → buyer receives professional communication.

STAGE 01 Sourcing

Carefully selected sourcing network

Identify suitable Indian producers and production capability with confirmed origin feedstock.

STAGE 02 Requirement

Requirement-led sourcing

Understand the buyer's application, technical specifications, volume, and commercial requirements.

STAGE 03 Inspection

Quality verification before export

Personally review supplier output before approval where practical to ensure lot standards.

STAGE 04 Quality Control

Non-conforming material is not approved

If material is not acceptable, request correction/reproduction or use another qualified producer.

STAGE 05 Coordination

One professional point of contact

Manage buyer communication, reasonable customization, packaging, and commercial coordination.

STAGE 06 Logistics

Responsible export coordination

Coordinate approved orders and disciplined export documentation, phytosanitary, and maritime logistics.
